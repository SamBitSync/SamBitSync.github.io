## Overview

An interactive storymap documenting digital infrastructure, access patterns, and community perspectives in Bhimdhunga, Nagarjun. It sets official coverage statistics against household-level stories (interviews, photographs, and spatial data) to show the gap between what the numbers say about connectivity and how residents actually experience it.

Built in a team during the All In Solutions fellowship, 2025.

## Approach

- **Mixed methods**: household interviews plus infrastructure mapping
- **Community-centered**: residents' own accounts drive the narrative, not extracted data points
- **Visual-first**: minimal text, multimedia story points on an interactive map
- **Offline-first**: plain HTML/CSS/JS with no external dependencies, so it can be demonstrated and shared in the low-connectivity areas it documents

## What the Stories Show

Coverage maps make Bhimdhunga look reasonably connected. The interviews complicate that: connectivity is intermittent, affordability and digital literacy constrain access more than infrastructure does, most internet use is mobile-only, and access runs through community and household networks more than individual subscriptions.

## Reflection

**What worked:** community participation gave the narrative its authority; the visual approach crossed language barriers; offline-first design meant residents could actually view and share the thing.

**What I'd change:** more quantitative visualization to pair with the stories, a temporal dimension (how access has changed), and a way for residents to update it without me.
