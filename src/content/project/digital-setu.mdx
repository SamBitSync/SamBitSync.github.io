We made Digital Setu during the All In Solutions fellowship in 2025. The map connects interviews, photographs, and videos with the places they came from in Bhimdhunga.

We combined household interviews with infrastructure mapping. Residents' accounts describe their connections, the costs of access, and the difficulties they encounter using digital services.
