# Sampling rates and temporal resolution

When 60Hz fails and when 1000Hz is overkill.

- **60Hz**: 1 sample every ~16.6ms.
- **1000Hz**: 1 sample every 1ms.

For reading studies, 1000Hz is crucial.
For VWP, where saccades take 30-50ms and fixations are 200ms+, is 60Hz enough?
Our data suggests 60Hz captures the *decision* timing adequately, but misses fine-grained saccade dynamics.
