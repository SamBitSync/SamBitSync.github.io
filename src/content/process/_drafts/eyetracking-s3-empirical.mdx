# What audio-visual alignment actually requires

If the audio lags the video by 50ms, our "anticipatory" looks might just be reactive.

**Measurement**:
- Photodiode on screen.
- Microphone on speaker.
- Oscilloscope to measure delta.

**Result**:
- Standard PC audio latency: ~30-80ms.
- ASIO drivers: &lt;10ms.
- PsychoPy `PTB` backend: &lt;10ms.

We *must* use low-latency audio drivers for VWP.
