# When eyes land on nothing

Sometimes the gaze coordinates are clearly "nowhere" - between objects, off-screen, or just drifting.

## The Gap
This represents the gap between *gaze* (mechanical) and *attention* (cognitive).
- Are they thinking?
- Are they transitioning?
- Is the tracker lost?

We need a strategy to handle "Null" looks without discarding valid data.
