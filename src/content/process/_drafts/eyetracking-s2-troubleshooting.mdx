# When participants look at edges, corners, or just zone out

Participants get bored. They look at the bezel. They stare at the blank space.

**Problem**: This data is "valid" (eyes were open) but "invalid" (not task-related).

**Strategy**:
- **Zone Out**: Long fixations (>2s) on nothing? Flag trial.
- **Corner Hugging**: Calibration drift? Or just resting?
- **Blinking**: Excessive blinking indicates fatigue.

We need heuristics to filter "low quality engagement" trials.
