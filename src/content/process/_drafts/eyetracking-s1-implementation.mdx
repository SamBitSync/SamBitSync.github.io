# Calibration as contract between ideal and actual gaze

Calibration isn't just a setup step; it's a mapping function.

We map:
`Raw Pupil/CR` -> `Screen Coordinates`

This mapping is non-linear and drifts over time.
Implementation:
- 9-point grid.
- Polynomial fit or TPS (Thin Plate Spline).
- Validation step to check error residuals.
