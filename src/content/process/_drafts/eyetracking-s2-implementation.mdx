# Screen geometry and the 2x2 grid that dominates the literature

Why is the 2x2 grid so common?

1.  **Maximizes Separation**: Corners are furthest apart.
2.  **Minimizes Central Bias**: No object is in the center (where gaze naturally rests).
3.  **Symmetry**: Equal saccade distance to all targets.

Implementation:
- 1920x1080 screen.
- 4 images (300x300px) centered in quadrants.
- Central fixation cross for drift check.
