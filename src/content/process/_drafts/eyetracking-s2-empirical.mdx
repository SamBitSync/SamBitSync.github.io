# What does VWP actually need?

To make the "time machine" work, we need:

1.  **Areas of Interest (AOIs)**: Clearly defined, well-separated.
2.  **Timing Constraints**: The visual scene must appear *before* the critical audio onset (preview period).
3.  **Competitor Selection**: Distractors must be carefully chosen (phonological, semantic, or visual competitors) to test specific hypotheses.

If the preview is too short, no prediction. If competitors are too salient, bottom-up capture.
