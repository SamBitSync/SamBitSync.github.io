# Remote deployment on Gorilla - what breaks and surprising workarounds

WebGazer.js (used by Gorilla) is not an EyeLink 1000.

**What breaks**:
- Precision drops to ~5-10% of screen width.
- Head movement kills calibration.
- Lighting conditions vary wildly.

**Workarounds**:
- **Huge AOIs**: 4 quadrants only. No small objects.
- **Virtual Chinrest**: Card calibration trick to estimate distance.
- **Shorter Blocks**: Recalibrate every 2 minutes.

It works for coarse VWP, but requires different design constraints.
