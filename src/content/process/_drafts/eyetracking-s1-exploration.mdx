# Is 2-3° precision adequate? Turns out that's the wrong question

We often chase sub-degree precision. But in VWP, our targets are often large images.

If my target is 10° wide, do I need 0.5° precision?
Or is 2-3° enough to reliably say "they are looking at the target"?

This exploration challenges the assumption that "more precision is always better" at the cost of other factors.
