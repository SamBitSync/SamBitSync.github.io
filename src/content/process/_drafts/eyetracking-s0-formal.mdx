# Saccades jump, fixations sample, pursuit tracks

Three movement types, three cognitive functions:

1.  **Saccades**: Ballistic movements. Once started, they cannot be changed. They reflect a *decision to move* attention.
2.  **Fixations**: The pauses between saccades. This is where visual intake happens. Duration correlates with processing difficulty.
3.  **Smooth Pursuit**: Tracking a moving object. Requires a target to lock onto.

In VWP, we mostly care about the interplay between 1 and 2. We measure *where* (saccade target) and *how long* (fixation duration).
