# Why show pictures when studying language processing

Language is auditory (mostly). Why bring vision into it?

The Visual World Paradigm (VWP) isn't just about "seeing." It's about **externalizing the mental model**.
By providing a visual context, we restrict the domain of reference.
This allows us to see *which* interpretation the listener is entertaining at any given millisecond.

It turns the hidden process of comprehension into an observable behavior (looking).
