# Continuous anticipatory looks reveal word-by-word comprehension unfolding

Language processing is incremental. We don't wait for the end of the sentence.

**Hypothesis**: As soon as information becomes available, the system updates its probabilistic model of the message.
**Mechanism**: This update shifts attention to the most likely referent in the visual scene.
**Observable**: Eye movements to the target *before* the target word is fully spoken.

This "time machine" effect allows us to study prediction in real-time.
